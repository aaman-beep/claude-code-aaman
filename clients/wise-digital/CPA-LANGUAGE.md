## 3. Three mechanisms that visibly landed

### a) "A faucet, not a fire hose" — the capacity objection, pre-handled
> *"it's a **faucet, not a fire hose** initially. So it's **easy to control the flow** once we get the foundation going"* — [1281]

George had already raised capacity: *"we have the capacity but we don't want [to be overwhelmed]"* — [1273]. Patrick had used the same frame earlier: *"we don't want to topple her with too much, fast growth"* — [1273].

This matters because your current T2 alt leans on *"it's tough to stay selective with clients"* — which pokes the same nerve but offers no relief. The faucet line **answers** it. Best single addition to the copy from this call.

> Strawman: "it's a faucet not a fire hose — you control the flow"

### b) Search leads beat referral leads *on quality*
> *"you get really **qualified leads, quality is way better** than — I'd argue — than you're even getting **through referrals**"* — [1277]

A genuine reframe. Every CPA believes referrals are the highest-quality lead there is; this attacks the belief rather than the volume. Pairs with the Dark Horse mechanism, explained concretely at [1276]: someone searching "Dark Horse CPAs" anywhere in the country gets routed to a principal, by design.

### c) The 44-year website
> *"a website that had **never generated a lead in 44 years**"* — [1260]

Concrete, absurd, instantly legible, and it's about *them* not you. Strong pattern-interrupt candidate for a T1.

> Strawman: "most CPA sites haven't produced a single lead in decades — ours took Dark Horse from $1.5m to $21m"
