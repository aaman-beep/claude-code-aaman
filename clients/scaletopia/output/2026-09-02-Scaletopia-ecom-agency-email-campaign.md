## FINAL COPY — spintaxed, EmailBison format

Spun to the same density and convention as Kynship's live campaign (1103, step 2542): merge tags
UPPERCASE, HTML `<p>` bodies with `<p><br></p>` between paragraphs, spin on greeting / connectives
/ verb phrases / sign-off, **proof and hook left fixed**.

`{FIRST_NAME}` replaces your "Name,". **Pick-0 renders your copy word for word** — nothing drifted,
except the relevance line now carries `{COMPANY}` as you asked:
*"Since I know Amsive also works with ecom brands…"*

`Since I know {COMPANY} also` is now **fixed, not spun** — every one of the 6,561 renders carries
the company name in that line. Only the verb rotates (`works with|partners with|serves`), which is
the same three your live campaign 988 uses.

**What I deliberately did NOT spin:** `But we've cracked cold outreach for Chamber Media` and
`AG1, Volcom and Feastables`. Kynship does the same — they spin everything around
`Purdy & Figg from GBP 452k to GBP 50m in 4 years` but never the proof itself. Spinning a winning
line just gives you three weaker versions of it.
