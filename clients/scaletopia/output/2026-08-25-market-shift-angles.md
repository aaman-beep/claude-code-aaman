## Why this structure is better than what's running

```
T1: [disarmer] → [market-shift claim] → [case study as EVIDENCE for the claim]
T2: [for {company} I'd reach out to {ICP_signal}] → [soft CTA]
```

The running copy leads with the brag (*"we just helped Chamber Media sign 18 retainers…"*), so
the reader's first job is to decide whether to care about us. Here the first thing they get is a
**claim about their market** — and the case study arrives subordinated to it, as the *"it's how"*.
Same proof, completely different read.

`levers.md` files this under FOMO → **"a market shift they haven't moved on"**: *"the way
[category] is being done now is X, most haven't switched."* That's the one FOMO trigger we can
fire honestly here — we have no rival to name and no credible scarcity.

**The claim is supportable.** LeadGenix positives-per-send: SMS beats email ~8×. WISE Digital:
**6 of 8 Won deals are `channel: sms`**, 87 sms deals vs 18 email across the account. We are not
inventing a trend — it's in our own numbers.

## The variants

### M2 · Trend + summer recency — *strongest*
```

    other channel for us right now. WISE Digital signed 3 retainers off it over the summer.
```

Trend claim + the freshest verified proof we hold. Both halves are checkable.

### M3 · Not-email contrast
```
T1: Hi {first_name}, feel free to ignore if outbound's sorted - but the thing working best for
    us right now is text, not email. WISE Digital signed 8 retainers off it since March.

T2: for {company} I'd reach out to {ICP_signal}. could I show you how?
```
Sharpest for this list specifically — these prospects were selected *because* they run cold
email. "text, not email" tells them the thing they already suspect about their own channel.

### M4 · "we're finding" — softest, most peer
```

    outreach is outperforming everything else rn. it's how WISE Digital signed 3 retainers
    over the summer.

T2: for {company} I'd reach out to {ICP_signal}. could I show you how?
```
"we're just finding" is a report, not a pitch. Lowest guard-spike of the five.

### M5 · Same-list contrast — ⚠️ needs your sign-off
