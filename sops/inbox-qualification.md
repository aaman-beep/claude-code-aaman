# Lead Qualification — Inbox Manager

**Default is QUALIFIED.** Only disqualify if something below actually fails.

---

## The 3 checks (~10 seconds)

| # | Check | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Job title** | `Title` field — already on your screen. *(Ignore `Job Title`, it's always empty.)* |
| 2 | **Employees + year founded** | Click their `LinkedIn profile` → their company → **About** |
| 3 | **Website works?** + traffic if ecom | Click `Website`. Traffic = Storeleads, or SEMrush free (use the **Organic Search** number) |

Most leads die on the title. Only run check 3 if checks 1 and 2 passed.

---

## When you disqualify — do BOTH

1. Set `Lead Categorisation` → **Disqualified**
2. Write the reason in `DQ Reason` — **with the number you saw**

**Why this matters:** every reason you write goes back to the team building the lists. Write *"Too small — 4 employees"* enough times and the headcount filter gets fixed — and you stop getting these replies in the first place. Leave it blank and nothing changes.

| Reason | Write it like this |
|---|---|
| Wrong function | `Wrong function — Product Marketing Manager` |
| Junior title | `Junior title — Marketing Coordinator` |
| Not the person | `Not the person — left the company` / `sold the business` |
| Wrong business type | `Wrong business — SaaS, not ecom` |
| Dead website | `Dead website — site doesn't load` |
| Wrong country | `Wrong country — Australia` |
| Too small | `Too small — 4 employees` |
| Too new | `Too new — founded 2023` |
| Franchise / PE | `Franchise — Renewal by Andersen, 200+ employees` |
| Low traffic | `Low traffic — 300/mo` |
| Can't check | `Couldn't check — no LinkedIn on record` |

Never just "too small" or "not a fit" with no number.

---

## Auto-DQ everywhere — wrong function

Same list for every client. This is about **what they own, not how senior they are.** A *Director* of Product Marketing still doesn't buy ads.

**The test:** *Could this person approve a $5k/month invoice for what this client sells?* If it needs someone else's sign-off → **DQ**.

**Never a fit — any client, any seniority:**
Product Marketing · Customer Success / Account Management · Community · Content / Copywriting / Design · Social Media · HR / Recruiting / Talent · IT / Engineering · Office Manager / Executive Assistant

**Too junior — any function:**
Coordinator · Specialist · Assistant · Intern · SDR / BDR

**Partnerships, Business Development and Sales are FINE.** Those are go-to-market roles and often the buyer or close to them. Don't DQ on those titles alone.

**Who does buy:** Owner · Founder · CEO · President · Partner · GM · CMO · VP / Head of / Director of — Marketing, Growth, Ecommerce, Acquisition, Performance, Retention, Partnerships, BD, Sales.

## Two exceptions

- **Sold in stores or on Amazon?** Low website traffic is fine — retail brands don't get much site traffic. If they pass employees + years, **keep them.**
- **Owner at a 200+ employee local business?** Usually franchise or private-equity owned (Renewal by Andersen, Bath Fitter, Jani-King). They can't buy. DQ. *Leadgenix, Wise Digital and Growth Lab only — for ecom clients a big brand is a good lead.*
