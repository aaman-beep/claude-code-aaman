# Report format (chat, not an artifact)

Headline line: `## <Day> <Mon DD>: <N> warm replies from <people> people, <B> booked`

Then, in this order:

**Sends** table: list / copy (short name) · people · texts · failed % · blank company. One row per first-text
template with 3+ people, plus email (sent per campaign, leads left).

**Replies (counted through <day>)** table: list · warm (per 1k people) · who (name, company, title, verbatim reply).
Under it one line each for: not leads, stops, no's. Then a one-line comparison with earlier days of the same list.

**Handling** bullets: per warm reply, what happened after (booked / times offered, no pick / handoff email only /
no reply). Lead with anything unanswered.

**Problems** (only if any): blank merge fields, wrong ICP signals that drew pushback, no-show texts to people
who joined, disqualified leads still on the calendar, lists running out, email at zero.

**Fix today**: 3-6 numbered items, most urgent first.

Length: one screen. No em dashes, no jargon, no "not X, it's Y".
